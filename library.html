Structures

Library

Get a structure from the game, in Structures then Online library. Or download it here, drop it in your structures folder and reload the list.

Banners

Banners for the top of the menu. Download them here, or straight from the game: Settings, then Banner library.

Share a banner

Sharing needs a free GitHub account. Your image is public as soon as you send it, and it joins the library once it has been reviewed. PNG up to 4096 pixels wide, shrunk to 1280 for the game, or GIF up to 600 pixels. 10 MB at most.

Add your structure

Choose a .nbt file captured with the trainer. It is checked in your browser and nothing is uploaded unless you share it.
