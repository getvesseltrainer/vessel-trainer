Become a partner

Run a Discord community or a Minecraft server? Show it here, next to the trainer.

What you get

  • Your card on this page, with your logo and your description in English and French.
  • Your server's live status: online, players on it, version.
  • Your IP, copied in one click, and a button to join your Discord.

What we ask

  • A Minecraft community that is welcoming and respectful.
  • A Discord invite link that does not expire.
  • A square logo, and a sentence or two about you.

How to apply

  1. Send your request on GitHub or write to us on Discord.
  2. We take a look at your community.
  3. Your card shows up here once it is accepted.

For server owners

Give your team the Admin tab

On a Fabric or Paper 26.3 server, your admins handle players, the world and events from the trainer's menu. The server checks every action.
