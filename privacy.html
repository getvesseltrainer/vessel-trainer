Privacy

What is kept when you sign in to share a structure or a banner, and how to remove it.

Without an account

The site sets no tracking cookies and shows no ads. Structures kept "in this browser" stay on your device.

Partners page

To show whether partner servers are online, the page asks the public service mcstatus.io for their status. Your browser contacts it directly, like any website, but nothing other than the partner server's address is sent to it.

In the game

The mod only contacts vesseltrainer.org for two things: showing the library when you open it, and checking at launch whether a new version is out. It sends nothing about you or your world, and the check can be turned off in Settings.

With a Discord account

  1. What is kept: your Discord id and name, the email address Discord passes on, and the files you send with their name, your description and the name you pick.
  2. Why: only to know who sent what, limit spam and show your name in the library. Nothing is sold or shared, and your email is never shown.
  3. Where: at Supabase, which hosts the accounts and the waiting files. Approved files are published on this site with the name you picked.
  4. Delete: you can withdraw a waiting file yourself under My files. To delete your account or a published file, write to us on the Vessel Trainer Discord server.
